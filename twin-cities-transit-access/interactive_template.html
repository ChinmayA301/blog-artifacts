How many jobs can you reach by transit in the Twin Cities?

A scaled-down rerun of the Accessibility Observatory's Access Across America transit method, built to learn it. Not original research. Each block group shows how many jobs are reachable by walking plus scheduled transit, averaged over every departure minute from 7:00 to 8:59 AM, compared with walking alone.
